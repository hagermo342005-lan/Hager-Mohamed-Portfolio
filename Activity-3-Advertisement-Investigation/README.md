# Activity 3: Digital Forensic Investigation of Advertisements Displayed in 6th of October District

**Digital Forensics | Cybersecurity Incident Investigation | Digital Evidence Analysis**

## Overview

This activity presents a digital forensic investigation study of an incident involving advertisements displayed on a digital billboard along the 26th of July Corridor, 6th of October City, Giza, on 29 October 2026.

The investigation focuses on understanding the incident, evaluating available information sources, identifying potential digital evidence, and developing a structured forensic investigation methodology.

## Investigation Scope

- Incident information gathering and source evaluation.
- Identification and classification of potential digital evidence.
- Evidence acquisition and preservation planning.
- Chain of custody documentation.
- Incident timeline development.
- Investigation hypotheses and technical analysis.
- Findings, limitations, and recommendations.

## Investigation Focus

The investigation considers the possible involvement of:

- Digital billboard management systems.
- Content Management Systems (CMS).
- Media players and display controllers.
- Network connectivity and remote access.
- Authentication and system activity logs.
- Digital content and configuration files.

## Investigation Methodology

The investigation follows a structured digital forensic approach:

1. Collect and evaluate incident-related information.
2. Identify potential sources of digital evidence.
3. Define appropriate acquisition and preservation procedures.
4. Establish documentation and chain of custody requirements.
5. Develop and assess possible incident hypotheses.
6. Document findings, evidence gaps, and recommendations.

## Evidence Status

The evidence items identified in this activity represent potential sources for investigation. Original system images, logs, and forensic evidence have not been independently acquired or verified unless explicitly documented in the investigation report.

## Expected Outcomes

- A documented incident investigation.
- An evidence identification matrix.
- An evidence preservation plan.
- A chain of custody template.
- A structured incident timeline.
- A technical assessment of possible attack scenarios.
- Forensic findings and recommendations.
